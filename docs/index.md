
# ⚡ Security+ Study Academy ⚡

### One day at a time. One concept at a time.

Welcome to **Security+ Study Academy**, a free cybersecurity learning program created by [ghostparticle404](https://github.com/ghostparticle404).

Our mission is to make cybersecurity education accessible, engaging, and technically rigorous.

Whether you're completely new to cybersecurity or preparing for the CompTIA Security+ certification, this academy is designed to help you develop a deeper understanding of how security works.

## 🛡️ What You'll Learn

Our curriculum follows the five CompTIA Security+ SY0-701 exam domains:

- **General Security Concepts**
- **Threats, Vulnerabilities, and Mitigations**
- **Security Architecture**
- **Security Operations**
- **Security Program Management and Oversight**

The complete course is planned to include **16 weeks, 80 lessons, and weekly diagnostic and review assessments**.

## 🧠 How We Teach

**We don't just memorize definitions.**

We explore how technologies work, why security principles matter, and how to apply them in realistic scenarios.

Every lesson includes:

- Detailed explanations and technical examples
- Analogies, humor, and memory hooks
- Guided note-taking
- Original exam-style practice questions
- Detailed answer explanations
- Hands-on exercises where appropriate
- Recommended cybersecurity videos

### 🎯 Before You Begin: Weekly Diagnostics

**You don't have to study something you already know.**

Each week begins with an optional, ungraded **20-question diagnostic assessment** designed to help you identify your strengths and knowledge gaps before studying.

The diagnostic covers all five lessons for the week, with four questions per topic.

After completing it, you can reveal:

- An answer key with detailed explanations
- Direct links to the lesson sections covering missed concepts
- A topic-by-topic scorecard
- Personalized recommendations for what to study, review, or potentially skip

If you already understand a topic and can explain your reasoning, you may choose to skip that lesson and focus on something new.

**The goal is to study smarter, not simply study more.**

### 📖 During the Week: Learn and Apply

Each week includes five comprehensive lessons that build your understanding through explanations, examples, guided notes, and practical exercises.

You can follow the lessons in order or create your own learning path based on your diagnostic results.

### ✅ After the Lessons: End-of-Week Reviews

Every week concludes with an optional review designed to help you evaluate what you've learned.

Each review includes:

- **20 new practice questions** covering the same five topics as the diagnostic
- **Detailed answer explanations** with links to relevant lesson sections
- **A topic-level scorecard** to identify remaining knowledge gaps
- **Five cumulative application questions** that connect concepts across multiple lessons
- **Reflection activities** to help you evaluate your understanding and plan your next steps

If you completed the diagnostic, you can compare your before-and-after scores to see where you've improved.

These reviews emphasize understanding and application—not just memorizing the correct answers.

### 🚀 Learn at Your Own Pace

**Diagnostic → Study What You Need → Review → Move Forward**

There are no grades, deadlines, or required scores to advance.

Whether you're completely new to cybersecurity or preparing to earn your CompTIA Security+ certification, the goal is the same:

**Develop the knowledge, confidence, and practical skills to understand cybersecurity—not just pass an exam.**
