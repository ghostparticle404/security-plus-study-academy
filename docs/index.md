# ⚡ Security+ Study Academy

### One day at a time. One concept at a time.

Welcome to **Security+ Study Academy**, a free cybersecurity learning program by [ghostparticle404](https://github.com/ghostparticle404).

Our mission is to make cybersecurity education accessible, engaging, and technically rigorous.

## 🛡️ What You'll Learn

Our curriculum follows the CompTIA Security+ exam domains:

- General Security Concepts
- Threats, Vulnerabilities, and Mitigations
- Security Architecture
- Security Operations
- Security Program Management and Oversight

## 🧠 How We Teach

We don't just memorize definitions.

We explore how technologies work, why security principles matter, and how to apply them in realistic scenarios.

Every lesson includes:

- Detailed explanations and technical examples
- Analogies, humor, and memory hooks
- Guided note-taking
- Original exam-style practice questions
- Detailed answer explanations
- Hands-on exercises where appropriate
- Recommended cybersecurity videos

## 📬 Study by Email

Choose your own learning pace:

- **3 days per week** — Monday, Wednesday, Friday
- **4 days per week** — Monday, Tuesday, Thursday, Friday
- **5 days per week** — Monday through Friday

Every subscriber begins with Lesson 1 and receives lessons in sequence.

The course is completely free.
