## 🎯 What You'll Learn

The curriculum follows the five CompTIA Security+ SY0-701 exam domains:

- General Security Concepts
- Threats, Vulnerabilities, and Mitigations
- Security Architecture
- Security Operations
- Security Program Management and Oversight

The complete academy is planned to include **16 weeks, 80 lessons, and weekly diagnostic and review assessments**.

## 🧠 Our Learning Philosophy

**We don't just memorize definitions.**

We explain **why concepts matter**, explore **how technologies work**, and practice applying knowledge to realistic security situations.

Every lesson combines:

- Detailed technical explanations and practical examples
- Analogies, humor, and memory hooks
- Guided notes and active learning
- Original Security+-style practice questions
- Detailed answer explanations
- Hands-on exercises where appropriate
- Recommended cybersecurity videos

### 🎯 Diagnose → Learn → Review

Each week follows a flexible learning pathway:

**1. Weekly Diagnostic — What do you already know?**

Begin with an optional, ungraded 20-question assessment covering the week's five topics.

Use your results to identify strengths, uncover knowledge gaps, and decide which lessons to prioritize or potentially skip.

**2. Five Lessons — Build your understanding**

Study the concepts you need through explanations, examples, guided practice, and hands-on activities.

**3. End-of-Week Review — Apply what you've learned**

Complete 20 new practice questions and five cumulative application scenarios.

Each review includes detailed explanations, targeted links to lesson sections, and opportunities to compare your progress with your diagnostic results.

**The goal is to study smarter, not simply study more.**
